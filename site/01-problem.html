The blind-success gap

Blocking an attack is only half the story.

A prevention failure that alerts is visible. A prevention failure that alerts nothing is a blind spot. “Not tested” is not a pass.

See the four axes →
01 / visible failure

Prevention gap

The control failed, but the blue side can see it fail and schedule a fix.

02 / blind success

Detection gap

Nothing alerted — and here the attack also worked, so you learn about it from the incident. The verdict is the same even when the attempt was blocked: silence is the gap.
